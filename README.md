 ❤️ Share4Hearts

# A Community-Based Donation Platform

Share4Hearts is a web-based donation platform that connects people who have
surplus or unused resources with individuals and organizations that are in need.

The platform aims to reduce resource wastage and encourage people to share
food, clothes, stationery, and other useful items with NGOs, orphanages,
old-age homes, and other community organizations.

🌐 **Live Demo:** https://sharecare-616af.web.app/

📂 **GitHub Repository:** https://github.com/Soujanya2005/Share4hearts

# 🎯 Problem Statement

Large amounts of usable food, clothes, stationery, and other resources are
often wasted because people do not have an easy way to connect with people
or organizations that need them.

Share4Hearts provides a digital platform that bridges this gap by allowing
donors to share available resources and helping receivers, volunteers, and
organizations discover donation opportunities.

# 💡 Solution

Share4Hearts provides a simple platform where users can:

- Register and log in
- Donate available resources
- Add details about their donations
- Upload donation-related images
- Browse available donations
- Connect donors with receivers
- Support NGOs and community organizations
