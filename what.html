How It Works

Our platform bridges the gap between donors and receivers with simple, efficient steps.

🎁

1. Register

Donors, NGOs, or volunteers register and verify their profiles through our simple onboarding system.

📦

2. Donate or Request

Donors list food, books, or clothes to share, while receivers can request what they need in real time.

🤝

3. Connect & Deliver

Once matched, donors and receivers coordinate seamlessly for pickup or delivery through our volunteers.
